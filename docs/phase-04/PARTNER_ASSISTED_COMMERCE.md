# Partner-Assisted Commerce

Partner identity and pickup location are derived from the authenticated Partner profile. Exact customer lookup, attested customer creation, assisted basket, State checkout, payment instructions, and owned Order history are self-scoped.

Partner attestation permits assisted ordering only. It does not verify customer email and does not enable direct customer login. Partner Orders are prepaid. Partners cannot alter prices, confirm payment, use POD, select another Partner, assign Runners, or inspect unrelated customers and Orders.
